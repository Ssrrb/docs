# Certificate chain

*Decisions · none*

## Purpose

The team chose one document to measure the executed work of a project. This document holds that reason. This document holds the options that the team rejected. This document creates no rule.

## The decision

The application holds one document of measurement: the advance certificate. The name `certificado de avance` names it.

One advance certificate measures the work executed during one period against budget lines. The certificate holds a period, at least one measurement line, and the evidence of each line.

The certificate can name a subcontract contract. Without a contract the certificate is the internal control of the project. With a contract the certificate also consumes the ceiling of that contract.

The chain of money for a subcontract reads:

```
contract → advance certificate → payment
```

The application holds no document whose only purpose is to declare how much remains payable.

## Ownership

The ECMS owns the certificate and the measurement. The ECMS creates the certificate.

The ERP owns the contract, the economic obligations, and the payment. The ERP reads an approved certificate that names a contract as an obligation that can be paid. The ERP creates no second certification.

The common core owns the identity, the events, and the inbox.

## Why

The team reviewed a prototype that held three models for this single business fact. One model measured with quantities and photos. One model held an evidence string and an amendment flag. One model held a period and a physical progress percentage. Only one of them was reachable from the interface. The other two were dead code.

The prototype also computed the executed amount of a budget line at the moment of creation, before any approval. A rejected measurement left the budget wrong with no way to repair it.

The team also found that the prototype accumulated the previous quantity of a budget line from every certificate, including drafts. A draft leaked into the next measurement.

A single document removes these three failure modes. One fact, one document, one state machine.

## Options that the team rejected

| Option | Why the team rejected it |
|---|---|
| An internal advance and a formal certificate as two documents | Two documents for one fact. The prototype held three and could not keep them consistent. |
| A payment certificate that groups advances and carries a `paid` state | The payment document already says what was paid. A second truth about the same money drifts. The name `certificado de pago` names no document in Obra Studio. |
| A `paid` state on the advance certificate | Same reason. The state duplicated the payment document. |
| An executed amount computed at creation | A rejected measurement left the budget wrong. The team derives every amount from approved documents only. |
| An accumulated quantity that sums drafts | A draft leaked into the next certificate. The team derives from approved documents only. |
| A closed list of event types in the inbox | A list of types in the common core places a variety in the core. The rule L0-06 forbids that. The team applies a universal rule instead. |

## The two accumulated quantities

The team rejected a single accumulated quantity. A budget line can receive work from the crew of the project and from a subcontractor at the same time. One number cannot answer both questions.

| Quantity | Belongs to | Answers |
|---|---|---|
| Measured quantity | The budget line | How much of the line has the project executed |
| Certified quantity | The relation between a contract and a budget line | How much of that line that `socio` has already certified |

Both quantities come from the same approved certificate. The application derives both and stores neither.

## Consequences

The document `docs/domain/certificates.mdx` holds the measurement rules. The document `docs/domain/subcontracts.mdx` holds the ceiling. The document `docs/domain/payments.mdx` holds the payment.

The prototype stored the evidence as a base64 data URL inside the certificate. The application stores evidence files inside the folder of the project. The document `docs/system/persistence.mdx` holds that rule.

# Work unit

*Domain · draft*

## Purpose

A work unit is a node of the tree of an `obra`. The name `unidad de trabajo` names this entity. The ECMS owns the work unit. The ECMS creates it, changes it, and deletes it.

The work unit tree is the structure of the work breakdown of the `obra`. The work unit tree is also the structure of cost. No second tree exists.

A work unit holds the economic view of its part of the work: the budget, the commitments, the execution, the measurement, and the share of the indirect costs that the budget spreads.

## Rules

### L1-16 — Root work unit

An `obra` must hold exactly one root work unit.

### L1-17 — Parent of a work unit

A work unit must have exactly one parent.

### L1-18 — Kind of a parent

The parent of a work unit must be a work unit or the root work unit of the `obra`.

### L1-19 — No cycle

The parent of a work unit must not be the work unit itself.

### L1-20 — Name of a work unit

A work unit must declare one name.

### L1-21 — Creation of a work unit

Only the ECMS must create a work unit.

### L1-22 — Change of a work unit

Only the ECMS must change a work unit.

### L1-23 — Deletion of a work unit

Only the ECMS must delete a work unit.

### L1-24 — Direct cost of a work unit

The direct cost of a work unit must equal the sum of the amounts of the budget lines that name that work unit.

### L1-25 — Committed amount of a work unit

The committed amount of a work unit must equal the sum of the committed amounts of the budget lines that name that work unit.

### L1-26 — Executed amount of a work unit

The executed amount of a work unit must equal the sum of the executed amounts of the budget lines that name that work unit.

### L1-27 — Measured amount of a work unit

The measured amount of a work unit must equal the sum of the measured amounts of the budget lines that name that work unit.

### L1-28 — Allocated indirect of a work unit

The allocated indirect of a work unit must equal the total indirect of the budget multiplied by the direct cost of the work unit and divided by the direct cost of the budget.

### L1-29 — No indirect when the budget has no direct cost

A work unit must hold no allocated indirect when the direct cost of the budget is zero.

### L1-30 — Total cost of a work unit

The total cost of a work unit must equal the direct cost of the work unit plus the allocated indirect of the work unit.

## Example

One budget spreads its indirect costs over its work units in proportion to the direct cost of each unit.

| Work unit | Direct cost | Share of the indirect | Total cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cimentación | 16,000,000.00 | 66.67% | 21,333,333.33 |
| Losas | 8,000,000.00 | 33.33% | 10,666,666.67 |

The sum of the shares is one hundred percent. The sum of the allocated amounts equals the total indirect of the budget.

## Notes

The allocated indirect of a work unit is derived. The application must not store it. The document `docs/system/persistence.mdx` holds that rule.

A budget line names at most one work unit. The document `docs/domain/budgets.mdx` holds that rule.

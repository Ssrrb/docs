# Payment

*Domain · draft*

## Purpose

A payment pays the work that an approved advance certificate certifies. The name `pago` names this entity. The ERP owns the payment.

A payment cancels all or part of the certified amount of one approved certificate. A payment records the method, the bank reference, the date, and the evidence of the transfer.

A registered payment never changes. A mistake is corrected by cancelling the payment with a reason and registering another one.

## Rules

### L1-128 — Creation of a payment

Only the ERP must register a payment.

### L1-129 — Certificate of a payment

A payment must name exactly one advance certificate.

### L1-130 — The named certificate must be approved

A payment must name an advance certificate that is in the state `aprobado`.

### L1-131 — Project of a payment

A payment must belong to the same project as its advance certificate.

### L1-132 — Amount of a payment

A payment must declare exactly one amount. The amount of a payment must be greater than zero.

### L1-133 — Method of a payment

A payment must declare exactly one method. The methods are `transferencia`, `cheque`, and `efectivo`.

### L1-134 — Reference of a payment

A payment must declare exactly one reference.

### L1-135 — Date of a payment

A payment must declare exactly one date.

### L1-136 — Evidence of a payment

A payment must hold zero evidence files or more.

### L1-137 — State of a payment

A payment must declare exactly one state.

### L1-138 — A registered payment never changes

A payment in the state `registrado` must not change.

### L1-139 — No deletion of a registered payment

The application must not delete a payment in the state `registrado`.

### L1-140 — Reason of a cancellation

The cancellation of a payment must record one reason.

### L1-141 — Hard ceiling on a payment

The application must not register a payment that would make the paid amount of a contract greater than the certified amount of that contract.

### L1-142 — Partial payment

A payment must cancel all or part of the certified amount of its advance certificate.

### L1-143 — A cancelled payment does not count

A cancelled payment must not contribute to the paid amount of a contract.

## Example

The states of a payment follow this order.

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | The user edits the payment. |
| Registered | The transfer happened. The payment counts. |
| Cancelled | The payment was wrong. It keeps its record and stops counting. |

## Example

A contract of 20,000,000.00 with 8,000,000.00 certified accepts payments until it reaches that amount.

| Payment | Amount | Paid amount of the contract | Available to pay |
|---|---|---|---|
| First | 5,000,000.00 | 5,000,000.00 | 3,000,000.00 |
| Second | 3,000,000.00 | 8,000,000.00 | 0.00 |
| Third | 1.00 | 8,000,000.00 | 0.00 |

The application refuses the third payment.

## Notes

A payment pays a certificate. The application holds no document whose only purpose is to declare how much remains payable. The document `docs/decisions/certificate-chain.mdx` holds that decision.

An account pays nothing. The application holds no payroll. A contract of category `M.O.` (labor) pays a `socio`, never a worker. The document `docs/domain/subcontracts.mdx` holds that contract.

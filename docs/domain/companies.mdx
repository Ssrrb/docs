# Construction company

*Domain · draft*

## Purpose

A construction company executes an `obra`. A construction company owns projects. A construction company owns user accounts.

The name `construction company` names an entity of the domain. The company holds no rule about the money of one project. A project holds the rules about the money of that project. The company holds the rules about an amount that crosses projects.

## Rules

### L1-01 — One company per project

A project must belong to exactly one construction company.

### L1-02 — One company per obra

An `obra` must belong to exactly one construction company.

### L1-03 — One company per user account

A user account must belong to exactly one construction company.

### L1-04 — Several accounts

A construction company must hold one user account or more.

### L1-144 — Scope of the company

A construction company must own the information that crosses its projects.

### L1-145 — Currency of an amount that crosses projects

An amount that crosses projects must keep the currency of the project that owns it.

### L1-146 — Total of every currency

A report that crosses projects must hold one total for every currency of the projects in the report.

### L1-147 — No conversion

A report that crosses projects must not convert an amount from one currency to another currency.

### L1-148 — Money that entered

The money that entered a currency must equal the sum of the registered collections of that currency.

### L1-149 — Money that left

The money that left a currency must equal the sum of the registered payments of that currency.

The cashflow report shows the two sums of a currency. The document `docs/product/mvp.mdx` holds the rule L3-32.

## Example

One construction company runs three projects. Two of them declare the dollar. One declares the guaraní. A cashflow report that crosses the three projects holds two rows.

| Currency | Money that entered | Money that left | Net |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dollar | 1,240,000 | 980,500 | 259,500 |
| Guaraní | 312,000,000 | 268,400,000 | 43,600,000 |

The report holds no row that sums the two currencies. The rule L1-147 holds the report from converting an amount. The rule L1-10 holds every amount in the currency of its project.

## Notes

The application serves one construction company in the MVP. The documents `docs/product/mvp.mdx` and `docs/execution/roadmap.mdx` state that scope.

Every account of a company holds the same capabilities. The document `docs/system/permissions.mdx` holds that rule.

The rule L1-144 gives the company the information that crosses its projects. The document `docs/product/mvp.mdx` shows that information in the company view.

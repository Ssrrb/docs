# Client

*Domain · draft*

## Purpose

The name `cliente` names this entity. A `cliente` is a person or a company that pays the work of a project. The ERP owns the record of the client. A project holds a list of clients. A collection names one client of that list.

## Rules

### L1-150 — Legal form of a client

A client must declare exactly one legal form. The legal forms are `persona` and `empresa`.

### L1-151 — Identifier of a client

A client must declare exactly one identifier.

### L1-152 — Name of a client

A client must declare one name.

### L1-153 — List of clients

A project must hold exactly one list of clients.

## Example

A project holds two clients. One client pays a certificate.

| Client | Legal form | Named by a collection |
|---|---|---|
| Helena Ruiz | `persona` | Yes |
| Constructora del Este | `empresa` | No |

A client is not a `socio`. The document `docs/domain/partners.mdx` holds the `socio` record.

## Notes

The list of clients belongs to the project. The document `docs/domain/collections.mdx` holds the collection that names a client.

# Socio

*Domain · draft*

## Purpose

A `socio` is a neutral record of a person or a company that the `obra` operates with. The record holds no role. A `socio` acts as a `proveedor` (supplier) when a purchase names it. A `socio` acts as a `subcontratista` (subcontractor) when a subcontract contract names it.

The same record carries both roles. A company that sells cement and that builds foundations is one `socio` in the list of the project. A person that works alone on a foundation is also one `socio`.

## Rules

### L1-60 — Legal form of a socio

A `socio` must declare exactly one legal form. The legal forms are `persona` and `empresa`.

### L1-61 — Identifier of a socio

A `socio` must declare exactly one identifier.

### L1-62 — Name of a socio

A `socio` must declare one name.

### L1-63 — List of socios

A project must hold exactly one list of `socio`.

### L1-64 — No role

A `socio` must not declare a role. The documents that name a `socio` give its role.

### L1-65 — Origin in a purchase order

A purchase order of a project must name exactly one `socio` of the list of that project.

### L1-66 — Role in a purchase

A `socio` must act as a `proveedor` when a purchase order names it.

### L1-67 — Origin in a subcontract contract

A subcontract contract of a project must name exactly one `socio` of the list of that project.

### L1-68 — Role in a contract

A `socio` must act as a `subcontratista` when a subcontract contract names it.

### L1-69 — No price at a socio

A `socio` must not declare the price of an input. The price list of a project holds the price of an input.

## Example

One record carries both roles. The role comes from the documents that name it.

| Socio | Legal form | Named by a purchase order | Named by a subcontract contract |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hormigón del Sur | `empresa` | Yes, acts as `proveedor` | Yes, acts as `subcontratista` |
| Acero Paraguayo | `empresa` | Yes, acts as `proveedor` | No |
| Fundación SRL | `empresa` | No | Yes, acts as `subcontratista` |
| Juan Pérez | `persona` | No | Yes, acts as `subcontratista` |

The record of Juan Pérez holds no category that says subcontractor. A subcontract contract says it.

## Example

A contract of category `M.O.` (labor) can name a company or a person.

| Category | Socio | Legal form |
|---|---|---|
| `M.O.` | Fundación SRL | `empresa` |
| `M.O.` | Juan Pérez | `persona` |

## Notes

The document `docs/domain/inputs.mdx` holds the price list of a project.

A legal form of `persona` receives the same payment document as a legal form of `empresa`. The application holds no payroll and no withholding. The document `docs/execution/roadmap.mdx` holds that work as deferred.

A longer list of legal forms is deferred work. Two forms cover the decisions that the application makes today.

The application holds no integration with a `socio`. The user enters a `socio`. The document `docs/execution/roadmap.mdx` holds the work for an integration with a price catalog.

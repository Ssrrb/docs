# Subcontract contract

*Domain · draft*

## Purpose

A subcontract contract hires a `proveedor` to execute work. The name `contrato de subcontratación` names this entity. The ERP owns the contract. The contract declares the maximum that the project can certify against that `proveedor`.

A subcontract contract covers one budget line or more. The contract holds a global ceiling. The control of the contract runs over the total certified amount, not over each covered line.

The category of a contract names the kind of work. The category is free text. The category `M.O.` (labor) names the contracts that hire labor.

## Rules

### L1-89 — Creation of a subcontract contract

Only the ERP must raise a subcontract contract.

### L1-90 — Proveedor of a subcontract contract

A subcontract contract must name exactly one `proveedor`.

### L1-91 — Contract amount

A subcontract contract must declare exactly one contract amount.

### L1-92 — Covered budget lines

A subcontract contract must cover at least one budget line of the project.

### L1-93 — Currency of a contract amount

The contract amount of a subcontract contract must use the currency of the project.

### L1-94 — Category of a subcontract contract

A subcontract contract must declare exactly one category. The category must be free text.

### L1-95 — State of a subcontract contract

A subcontract contract must declare exactly one state.

### L1-96 — Certificates need a current contract

A subcontract contract must accept advance certificates only while it is in the state `vigente`.

### L1-97 — A closed contract keeps its history

A closed subcontract contract must keep its certified amount and its paid amount.

### L1-98 — Record of an increase

An increase of a subcontract contract must record the previous amount and the new amount.

### L1-99 — Certified amount of a contract

The certified amount of a subcontract contract must equal the sum of the amounts of the approved advance certificates that name that contract.

### L1-100 — Available to certify

The available amount to certify of a subcontract contract must equal the contract amount minus the certified amount.

### L1-101 — Paid amount of a contract

The paid amount of a subcontract contract must equal the sum of the registered payments that cancel its approved advance certificates.

### L1-102 — Available to pay

The available amount to pay of a subcontract contract must equal the certified amount minus the paid amount.

### L1-103 — Hard ceiling on approval

The application must not approve an advance certificate when the approval would make the certified amount of its contract greater than the contract amount.

## Example

One contract covers three budget lines and holds one global ceiling.

| Covered budget line | Certified against this contract |
|---|---|
| 2.1 Hormigón en losas | 4,000,000.00 |
| 2.2 Encofrado de losas | 2,500,000.00 |
| 2.3 Armadura de losas | 1,500,000.00 |

The certified amount of the contract is 8,000,000.00.

## Example

The states of a subcontract contract follow this order.

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | The user edits the contract. |
| Current | The contract accepts advance certificates. |
| Closed | The contract accepts no more certificates. It keeps its history. |

## Notes

An increase of the contract amount is the only way to certify above the current ceiling. The document `docs/domain/certificates.mdx` holds the certificate that consumes the ceiling.

The category of a contract is free text. A closed list of categories in the platform would place a variety in the core. The rule L0-06 of `docs/foundations/principles.mdx` forbids that.

# Certificates

*Domain · draft*

## Purpose

An internal advance measures work that the project has done. The name `avance interno` names this entity. A payment certificate asks a supplier for money. The name `certificado de pago` names this entity.

The ECMS issues an internal advance. The ERP issues a payment certificate. An internal advance belongs to one work unit. A payment certificate pays approved internal advances.

## Rules

### L1-77 — Creation of an internal advance

Only the ECMS must issue an internal advance.

### L1-78 — Work unit of an internal advance

An internal advance must belong to exactly one work unit.

### L1-79 — Content of an internal advance

An internal advance must measure at least one budget line.

### L1-80 — State of an internal advance

An internal advance must declare exactly one state.

### L1-81 — Creation of a payment certificate

Only the ERP must issue a payment certificate.

### L1-82 — Content of a payment certificate

A payment certificate must hold at least one approved internal advance.

### L1-83 — State of a payment certificate

A payment certificate must declare exactly one state.

### L1-84 — Payment of a payment certificate

The payment of a payment certificate must close the payment certificate.

### L1-85 — No payment before approval

A payment certificate that is not approved must not be paid.

## Example

The states of an internal advance follow this order.

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | The user edits the internal advance. |
| Issued | The user issued the internal advance to the work. |
| Approved | The user confirmed the measurement of the internal advance. |

The states of a payment certificate follow this order.

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | The user edits the payment certificate. |
| Issued | The user issued the payment certificate to the supplier. |
| Approved | The user confirmed the payment certificate. |
| Paid | The user paid the payment certificate. |

## Notes

The application holds no separation of duties. One user account issues and approves. The document `docs/product/mvp.mdx` states the scope of the MVP. The document `docs/execution/roadmap.mdx` holds the work for a second role.

The states of a purchase request are not defined. The document `docs/execution/roadmap.mdx` holds that work as pending.

The approval of an internal advance adds its measured amount to the budget. The document `docs/domain/budgets.mdx` holds that rule.

# Advance certificate

*Domain · draft*

## Purpose

An advance certificate measures the work that the project executed during one period. The name `certificado de avance` names this entity. The ECMS owns the certificate. The ECMS owns the measurement.

An advance certificate holds one period, at least one measurement line, and the evidence of each line. A measurement line names one budget line, the quantity of the period, the unit price of that budget line, and the amount.

An advance certificate can name a subcontract contract. Without a contract the certificate is the internal control of the project. With a contract the certificate also consumes the ceiling of that contract.

One document measures the work. The application holds no second document for the same fact.

## Rules

### L1-108 — Creation of an advance certificate

Only the ECMS must issue an advance certificate.

### L1-109 — Work unit of an advance certificate

An advance certificate must belong to exactly one work unit.

### L1-110 — Contract of an advance certificate

An advance certificate must name at most one subcontract contract.

### L1-111 — The named contract must be current

An advance certificate must name a subcontract contract that is in the state `vigente`.

### L1-112 — Period of an advance certificate

An advance certificate must declare exactly one period. A period must declare one start and one end. The end of a period must be later than its start.

### L1-113 — Lines of an advance certificate

An advance certificate must hold at least one measurement line.

### L1-114 — Budget line of a measurement line

A measurement line must name exactly one budget line of the project.

### L1-115 — Covered line

Every measurement line of an advance certificate that names a contract must name a budget line that the contract covers.

### L1-116 — Period quantity of a measurement line

A measurement line must declare exactly one period quantity.

### L1-117 — Unit price of a measurement line

The unit price of a measurement line must equal the unit price of the budget line that the line names.

### L1-118 — Amount of a measurement line

The amount of a measurement line must equal the period quantity of the line multiplied by the unit price of the line.

### L1-119 — Amount of an advance certificate

The amount of an advance certificate must equal the sum of the amounts of its measurement lines.

### L1-120 — Certified quantity of a contract line

The certified quantity of a subcontract contract over a budget line must equal the sum of the period quantities of the approved advance certificates of that contract that measure that line.

### L1-121 — Evidence of a measurement line

A measurement line must hold zero evidence files or more.

### L1-122 — State of an advance certificate

An advance certificate must declare exactly one state.

### L1-123 — Effect of a rejection

The rejection of an advance certificate must return the certificate to the state `borrador`.

### L1-124 — Record of a rejection

The rejection of an advance certificate must record the account that rejected it and the moment of the rejection.

### L1-125 — No deletion outside the draft

The application must not delete an advance certificate that is not in the state `borrador`.

### L1-126 — Effect of the approval on a contract

The approval of an advance certificate that names a contract must add its amount to the certified amount of that contract.

### L1-127 — Tax in the total

The total of an advance certificate must include the tax rate of the project.

## Example

The states of an advance certificate follow this order.

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Draft | The user edits the certificate and its lines. |
| Issued | The user issued the certificate to the work. |
| Approved | The user confirmed the measurement. The certificate measures the budget and certifies its contract. |

The certificate holds three states. A rejection is not a state. A rejection from `emitido` returns the certificate to `borrador`.

## Example

One certificate holds the measurement of one period. The accumulated quantity of a line comes from the approved certificates.

| Budget line | Unit price | Previous | Period | Accumulated |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 Hormigón en losas | 180,000.00 | 20.00 | 12.00 | 32.00 |
| 2.3 Armadura de losas | 95,000.00 | 4.00 | 3.00 | 7.00 |

The previous quantity holds the measured quantity of the budget line. The accumulated quantity holds the measured quantity of the budget line after this certificate is approved.

## Example

One budget line can receive work from the crew of the project and from a subcontractor. Two accumulated quantities answer two different questions.

| Budget line | Measured quantity | Certified against the contract of Hormigón del Sur |
|---|---|---|
| 2.1 Hormigón en losas | 32.00 | 20.00 |

The measured quantity belongs to the budget line. The certified quantity belongs to the relation between the contract and the budget line.

## Notes

The previous quantity and the accumulated quantity of a measurement line are derived. The application must not store them. The document `docs/system/persistence.mdx` holds that rule.

The approval of a certificate refuses to pass the ceiling of its contract. The document `docs/domain/subcontracts.mdx` holds that rule.

The ERP reads an approved certificate that names a contract as an obligation that can be paid. The ERP creates no second certification. The document `docs/domain/payments.mdx` holds that payment.
